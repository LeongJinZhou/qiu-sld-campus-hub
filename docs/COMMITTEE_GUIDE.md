# Campus Hub — Committee Guide

For club Presidents, Vice Presidents, Secretaries, Treasurers, committee members and advisors.

You never type what the university already knows. Your name, student ID, programme, year group, email and position are filled in for you and locked. Club and event details carry over from one form to the next.

## Your club page

Open your club from **Clubs**. As committee you see extra buttons: **New event proposal, Post / story, Members, Finance, Payment review** (Treasurer), **Meetings, AGM, Venues**. Requests from SLD appear at the top of the page — reply there within 7 working days.

## Running an event, start to finish

1. **Propose it** (**New event proposal**). Fill the 06 Event Proposal: summary, objectives, activities, itinerary, event committee, budget. CCA hours are worked out from the event length (1 hour = 1 CCA) and you can change them.
   - Submit at least **1.5 months** ahead (under 60 participants) or **3 months** ahead (60 or more). The date picker enforces this, and you'll get a reminder a week before your last day.
   - The event committee needs its own President, Vice President, Secretary and Treasurer.
2. **Sign and submit.** Draw your signature with a mouse or finger. The tracker then shows where the proposal is and whose approval it is waiting for: Advisor → (Dean for faculty events) → SLD → Assistant Registrar → Registrar → Bursar → COO.
   - If it is **returned**, fix what was asked and resubmit; earlier approvals are kept unless you changed the budget or dates.
3. **When approved**, the event is posted to the feed, the poster checklist and QR attendance unlock, and you can request funds.
4. **Poster.** Tick every item on the checklist (logos, title, date, time, venue, registration, CCA, contact) and upload it. Use real QIU photos only.
5. **Outdoor / off-campus?** Everyone attending must sign the indemnity form; signing closes 3 days before the event. People who haven't signed get a reminder, and cannot clock in.
6. **On the day.** Show the rotating QR on a screen; participants scan to clock in and out. You can also scan their profile QR or type a student ID.
7. **Afterwards.** Within **1 week**: finance record and receipts. Within **2 weeks**: post mortem, attendance list, survey, reel. The post mortem header (participants, committee) fills itself; AI can suggest improvements. Reminders are automatic.

## Money (Treasurer)

- **Fee.** Set the membership fee for the session; SLD approves it.
- **Online payments.** Students upload a receipt. AI reads the amount, date, reference and payee and confirms it automatically when everything matches. Anything unclear lands in **Payment review** with the AI reading beside the image.
- **Cash.** The student picks you, you accept, both tap ready, their screen shows a one-time QR, you take one photo of the QR and the cash, then both tap done. The receipt goes to both of you.
- **Ledger.** SLD enters the opening balance first. Every expense needs the original receipt (not a bank screenshot). Export the 02 Finance Record any time.
- **Fund withdrawal (07).** Items come from the approved budget. For events, request right after approval; for weekly activities, at least 2 weeks ahead. Chain: Secretary → President → Advisor → Assistant Registrar → Bursar → COO.

## Meetings and minutes (Secretary)

Create a meeting (in person, or online with a Google Meet link from your own QIU calendar — connect it once). After the meeting, jot notes; AI can turn them into the 13 Minutes, which you then edit, sign and submit (President → Advisor → SLD). For a weekly activity, take attendance too — CCA is awarded when the minutes are approved.

## Members

**Members** has search, filters, multi-level sort, saved views and Excel/PDF export (including the 10 Full Member List). Memberships last 365 days, then 7 days of grace with reminders. There is no refund and no leave button.

## Venues

Book a La Place booth (agree to the table rules), a semester venue, or a big room at least 1 week ahead. SLD confirms in the app.

## AGM season

When SLD opens the AGM period, your club page shows a countdown.

1. **Schedule the AGM** (venue or Google Meet). Every member is invited.
2. **Attendance.** Show the QR at the AGM; members scan it (it opens 1 hour before).
3. **Election.** Set the nomination and voting windows. Members nominate themselves (they must be able to serve the full July–June term) and vote once per position. Publish results after voting closes. Winners take over on 1 July.
4. **AGM Report (01).** AI drafts objectives, the opening speech and the agenda from your session; you need at least 2 objectives. Attendance, the current committee, the election and appointments fill themselves. Secretary signs → President → Advisor → SLD.
5. **Annual Activities Report (12).** AI writes each event's details (edit them), pick a theme, generate PPTX and PDF. Photos come from each event's gallery album.
6. **Submit the package**: AGM Report, Financial Report, Full Member List and Annual Activities Report together.

## Your profile

Your lifelong profile shows your clubs, positions, events and CCA hours, and has a personal QR anyone can scan. Hide any section in **Edit profile**. Download **certificates** for each committee position and each event you attended. Before you graduate, add a **personal email** so you can keep signing in as alumni.
